What happens when CareKoala reads something

A small AI model on the PC gives the screen text a score from 0 to 10. The score decides what happens next.

  1. 012345 0 to 5

    Nothing is sent. CareKoala keeps reading, one screen at a time.

  2. 67 6 or 7

    The guardian’s phone gets a check-in request.

  3. 8910 8 to 10

    The PC pauses behind a check-in screen until the guardian unlocks it.

A low score doesn’t prove a conversation is safe, and a high score can be a false alarm. CareKoala is a prompt to talk, not a diagnosis, and it is not an emergency service.

Set up in four steps

Both devices need internet access during pairing.

  1. Install CareKoala on the PC

    Open the installer you downloaded and follow it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway. Installing takes a few minutes.

  2. Install Guardian on your phone

    Download the Android app, open the file, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. Then allow notifications.

  3. Pair the PC and the phone

    On the PC, open Guardian and create a pairing invitation. On the phone, open Add person, then scan the QR or enter the code.

  4. Approve the guardian

    On the PC, verify the guardian name and select Approve guardian. Keys are exchanged automatically. Use the notification and lockdown tests to verify the connection.

Installing, step by step

Both apps come from this page rather than an app store, so your device may ask you to confirm.

Windows PC

  • The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. Choose More info, then Run anyway, but only after the checksum matches.
  • If the PC is missing the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, the installer adds it and Windows asks your permission once.
  • CareKoala installs for your Windows account. Domain protection is optional and asks for administrator approval only when you turn it on.
  • To remove it, open Settings, then Apps, then CareKoala. Your pairing is kept, so reinstalling reconnects.

Android phone

  • Set a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) first. Guardian asks for it before it shows or sends an unlock key.
  • Open the downloaded file. If Android says your browser can’t install apps, tap Settings, allow it, and go back.
  • Google Play Protect may warn about apps from outside the Play Store. Choose Install anyway if the checksum matches.
  • Tap Start receiving and keep notifications on. Battery saver and Force stop can delay check-ins until you open Guardian again.
  • Replacing a test build? Uninstall it first, then pair again. Test builds were signed with a different key.

What stays on the PC, and what reaches the phone

No accounts, no cloud processing, and no analytics.

Stays on the PC

  • Screenshots, deleted after text is read
  • The text on the screen, and your conversations
  • The AI model and every score it gives

Travels, encrypted

  • To the phone: a “please check in” request with the time
  • To the phone, when the PC pauses: a one-time unlock key only that phone can read
  • Back to the PC: that key, when the guardian taps Send unlock key to PC

Messages pass through the free ntfy.sh relay. It sees when a message is sent and how big it is, never what it says. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Known limits

  • The phone doesn’t get a confirmation that the PC unlocked.
  • The current model looks for harassment, threats, hate speech, self-harm and distress. It doesn’t yet flag scams such as requests for one-time passwords.
  • The check-in screen is not a lock. Ctrl+Alt+Delete and other Windows security tools stay available on purpose.

Questions

Does CareKoala send messages or screenshots anywhere?

No. Text recognition and the AI model both run on the PC. The only things that leave it are the encrypted check-in request and, when the PC pauses, an encrypted one-time unlock key for the guardian’s phone.

Why is the Windows download so large?

It carries its own AI model (about 800 MB), text recognition and the software they run on. That is what lets CareKoala work without sending anything to the cloud.

Can the person using the PC close the check-in screen?

It covers every monitor and ignores the usual close keys, but it isn’t a lock. Ctrl+Alt+Delete and Windows security tools stay available on purpose:

What if the PC is offline when I tap Send unlock key to PC?

The key can’t reach it. In Guardian, tap Reveal latest unlock key and read it to the person at the PC, who types it on the check-in screen.

Do I need an account?

No. The two apps pair with a private code that you move from the PC to the phone yourself.

Does it work on a Mac, iPhone or Linux?

Not yet. CareKoala runs on Windows PCs, and the guardian app runs on Android phones.

Is CareKoala an emergency service?

No. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
